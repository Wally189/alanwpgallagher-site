Privacy

How this website handles personal data.

This is a small personal and professional website. It does not run user accounts, ecommerce, comments or a mailing list.

The main way personal data may be provided is through the contact form, where you choose to send a name, email address and message.

Last updated: June 2026.

Data Handling Summary

What is collected and why.

Contact form — messages are delivered through Formspree.
Data provided — name, email address and message text.
Purpose — to read and respond to direct enquiries.
Retention — correspondence is kept only as long as needed for the enquiry and basic records.

Additional Notes

Third-party services and rights.

The website may involve normal technical processing by hosting and form-delivery services. The contact form is provided by Formspree, so messages submitted through the form are processed by that service before reaching me.

Please do not send confidential, sensitive or third-party personal information through the form.

You may ask for access, correction or deletion of personal data you have sent through the contact route. Use the Contact page for that request.
