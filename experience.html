Chapter II

Career evidence across technical, operational and regulated roles.

My career spans information security operations, technical support, business analysis, commercial administration, customer-sensitive services and NHS workflow support.

The private-sector bridge between Pramerica and Optum remains central: access governance, service operations, hardware lifecycle and cross-functional delivery under pressure.

II

Core Record

Steady growth in scope, trust and governance responsibility.

Pramerica Systems (2012–2016): information security operations with access management, role-based controls, recertification, ISO-aligned governance and audit-ready documentation, including Office 365 transition support.

Optum (2016–2019): senior technical support across hardware product portfolios, ServiceNow and Ariba exceptions, and close liaison with Engineering, Security, ISO teams, vendors and executive offices.

Optum (2019–2022): business analyst and associate technical support work covering procurement, logistics, forecasting and lifecycle control, including operational delivery through Brexit transition and pandemic remote-working deployment.

South West Truck & Van / Motability Operations (2022–2024): commercial administration, purchase orders, logistics, compliance records, lease administration and customer-sensitive case handling.

North Bristol NHS Trust (2024–present): regulated healthcare workflow support with confidential records, reporting, governance documentation and operational liaison across busy services.

Career Arc

Context that shaped the work.

One period I remain particularly proud of was supporting large-scale hardware delivery during the early Covid-19 remote-working shift while organisations were also navigating Brexit-related operational change. It was practical, detailed and demanding work: coordinating procurement, logistics, records and communication at pace without losing control of standards.
