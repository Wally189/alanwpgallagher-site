Data protection context.

This page summarises the data protection position for this small personal and professional website.

The site is intended to collect as little personal data as possible. The main personal data route is the contact form, used for direct enquiries.

Last updated: June 2026.

Your Rights

Access, correction and deletion.

Access — you may ask what personal data you have sent through the contact route.
Correction — you may ask for inaccurate contact details or message records to be corrected.
Deletion — you may ask for personal data from your enquiry to be deleted where appropriate.
Contact — use the Contact page for data-protection requests.
