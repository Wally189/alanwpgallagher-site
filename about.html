About

Bristol, Donegal, and the spaces between.

Bristol by birth, strongly shaped by Donegal, and used to moving between places and types of people, I have always been interested in how place, language and community shape the way people think and work.

Outside work I build small websites, study Irish and Spanish, cook, garden, and stay involved in parish and community life. That mix — digital, practical and human — feeds into how I approach work: clear structure, careful records and useful follow-through.

Personal Note

Practical by temperament, reflective by habit.

I have moved around the UK and Ireland over the years, and I have usually found that the most useful systems are the ones people can actually live with.

One of my favourite jobs in earlier years was running a busy Highland hotel for two years. It taught me a great deal about pace, standards, people, and the quiet art of keeping things moving when everything arrives at once.

I remain interested in language, place, practical stewardship and community. Those are personal interests, but they are also part of how I stay grounded in professional life.
