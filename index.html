I've been thinking about creating a digital space for a long time and 2026 is the year I have finally got around to doing it. I really wanted to cultivate a space which is a little bit about me and a "lot a bit" about my way of life. A professional and personal digital commonplace book of sorts. As with life, it is a work in progress, so you may find some pages are not quite as I would like them. I aim to have this resolved by February 2026.

As for me; I'm an ideas person, and someone who thinks in the layers of systems and methods, pretty much in all aspects of my life. As you can probably tell I'm also a tiny bit Catholic and that has a profound influence on my life - my raison d'etre and what I am about. That isn't to say in the professional environment I'm going to be thumping a bible out at every step - although my current boss periodically seems to see it as a challenge for me to try and convert her which often has me in fits of laughter. I'm just not that kind of Catholic! In fact, I was not always Catholic, I have lived an extra-ordinary life which I am sure I will reference at some point or other.

So what is this?

Some things here are practical: professional material, my CV, and a simple way to get in touch. There are other layers too - ideas, projects, and the strands of my life that shape how I live and work. On the back end it's an opportunity to play with the world of web development which is exciting for me too.

With experience, I have found that I am drawn to order, method, and work done carefully and well. These instincts are often dressed up in different ways such as Kaizen, Lean, Agile in the workplace (amongst other things) or repackaged in self-help language in the personal sphere. I've used some of these approaches, and still do. Today, my work is grounded in ways of thinking that are much older, and in my view, more proven.

I'm interested in slow technology: tools and systems that protect time, dignity, and clarity. I've used some of these approaches, and still do. Today, my work is grounded in ways of thinking that are much older, and in my view, more proven. A lot of my lifes work is now based on Monastic principles. I spent time really reviewing my entire life and the question became 'What systems just work?'. A nearly 2000 year old system, regardless of religious point of view seemed to me to demonstrably work so I thought 'How do I make this part of my own way of being?' and here we are...

I'm also interested in slow technology: tools and systems that protect time, dignity, and clarity. That is not to say I'm not modern. I love playing with new software, tools and resources. I'm a massive advocate of artificial intelligence for example. However; as with everything in my own life I like to apply my personal ruleset. I call it the WayLight ruleset - it's centred in the rule of St Benedict but seen through a modern lens and actually for the secular world.

I am not a monk or a Priest. Perhaps in a different life that may have been the case. I am; however, on a bit of a life mission. You are most welcome to join me....
