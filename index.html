Introduction

Mise Aileán – I am Alan

I have always been curious by default, a spotter of patterns, healthily sceptical, quietly strategic — and open to what comes next. I like to fuse old and new ideas, understand methodologies and to learn. I like to think things through and notice details. I also like to question assumptions - what if, what then, why and how? Over the years I’ve worked in many environments — often where clarity mattered but wasn’t always present yet through it all I have learnt and improved. I tend to map what’s happening, make sense of it, and improve what I can - like a cartographer of the mind.

In life, I have worn a few different hats and that breadth has been useful. It’s taught me perspective, resilience, and how to connect dots that aren’t obviously related.

Outside of work, I read widely, experiment with technology, and spend as much time as possible outdoors — particularly in the garden. I am a man of faith which is hugely important to me, and that shapes how I think about responsibility, integrity, and long-term direction.

A Bristolian by birth, I’ve always felt a strong connection to the city and its culture. I’m also Irish, and have spent a quarter of my life in County Donegal. I’m the eternal foreigner in both places, splitting my time between them. The end goal, amusingly enough, is Asturias in Spain.

Principles & perspectives

Selected strengths.

Order before speed – I prioritise structure before acceleration.
Clear processes, documented decisions and defined responsibilities prevent confusion later.
Calm under pressure – In time-critical or regulated environments, steadiness matters more than theatrics.
I focus on clarity, accuracy and follow-through.
Practical Modernism with traditional mindset – I embrace modern tools — including AI — where they enhance clarity and efficiency.
Technology should support judgement, not replace it. I also firmly believe in not reinventing the wheel.
Long term strategist – I am interested in sustainability — in systems, in work, and in life.
Well-built foundations create freedom over time.
