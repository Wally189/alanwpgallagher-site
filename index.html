Who am I?

Operationally grounded, governance-aware, and reliable in delivery.

I am a Bristol-based Information Security Specialist, Project Manager, Administrator and Operations professional with over twenty five years experience across Healthcare, Information Security, IT support, Legal Administration, and other regulated service environments.

I work best where accuracy, discretion and continuity matter. My approach is practical and structured: support delivery under pressure, keep documentation clear, and maintain standards teams can trust. I am a strong advocate for clear communication and consistent delivery in complex environments. A team player with a strong sense of responsibility and accountability. A natural influencer and leader of people.
