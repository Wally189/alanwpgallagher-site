# House of Carol — Public-safe independent-project summary

**Status:** Public case-study summary approved for publication; no claim of commercial performance.

**Public basis:** https://houseofcarol.co.uk/ and https://github.com/Wally189/house-of-carol.

A personal independent business-building project covering brand and public communication, website and catalogue design, and the development of structured methods for organising work. Public-facing materials can be examined at the website and public repository.

## Demonstrated publicly
- Existence of a public website and repository.
- Public explanations of the business proposition, service catalogue and customer-facing routes at the time of review.
- Source-controlled public-facing website development.

## Not established by these materials
- Paying-customer count, sales, profit or independently validated outcomes.
- The operation of all internal departmental roles as distinct people.
- External certification, product endorsements, insurance scope or verified customer results.

## Protected and not included
Private governance records, internal prompts, confidential infrastructure, House code, unpublished product details, customer data and business-sensitive strategy.
