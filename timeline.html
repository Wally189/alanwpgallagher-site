Timeline

Chronology of roles, responsibility and sector range.

The timeline below reflects over twenty years of operational work across healthcare, information security, technical support, customer operations, legal administration and related service environments.

It shows consistent progression in responsibility, governance awareness and cross-functional coordination.

The moment I am most proud of in my career is the successful delivery of critical hardware equipment over a period of many months during the early stages of the pandemic, which was a key part of enabling remote working for thousands of people across the USA, Brazil, Argentina, Australia, Germany and the UK and Ireland amongst other groups. This was in tandem with managing change due to the Brexit processes and fear of the unknown. As much as I loved this work, it was then I decided to take a step back for a while - which I have done. Now I am very fortunate as I am able to focus on the things I want to do.

Career Timeline

Year ranges and chapter context.

  1. 1999-2012

    Early career across UK and Ireland in telecommunications, hospitality supervision, legal claims administration, Civil Service-related and technical operations roles.

  2. 2012-2016

    Pramerica Systems - Information Security Associate (Operations Representative), providing Tier I and II access management, governance support, role-based controls and recertification activity.

  3. 2016-2019

    Optum - Senior Technical Support Specialist, managing hardware product portfolios, ServiceNow and Ariba exceptions, and collaborating with Engineering, Security and ISO teams.

  4. 2019-2022

    Optum - Business Analyst / Associate Technical Support Analyst, leading procurement, logistics and lifecycle work while supporting Brexit and pandemic remote-working programmes.

  5. 2022-2024

    Sales and customer operations roles at South West Truck & Van and Motability Operations, covering lease administration, compliance documentation, reporting and customer resolution work.

  6. 2024-Present

    North Bristol NHS Trust - Band 4 Administrator and Operations Support across Cardiac Testing, IPC and Tissue Viability, including high-volume workflow support, audit preparation and governance reporting.
