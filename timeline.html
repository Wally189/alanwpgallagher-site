Timeline

Professional chronology.

The timeline below reflects more than twenty years across information security operations, technical support, business analysis, customer operations, legal administration and healthcare workflow support.

One period I remain proud of was supporting critical hardware delivery during the pandemic remote-working push while organisations were also adapting to Brexit-related operational change. The work required tight coordination across procurement, logistics, records and communication, at scale and at pace.
