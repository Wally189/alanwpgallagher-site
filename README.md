# Alan W Gallagher Site

Professional portfolio and CV website for Alan W Gallagher.

This repository supports a public-facing portfolio focused on business and technical operations, service improvement, governance, systems support, digital administration, and responsible AI use.

It is not intended to present Alan as a conventional software developer. Its purpose is to show that he can understand digital tools, structure information clearly, translate between technical and non-technical audiences, and use modern tools responsibly to support practical decision-making.

## Portfolio position

- **Classification:** Portfolio
- **Primary audience:** hiring managers, recruiters, collaborators, and organisations looking for operational, governance, project-support, service-improvement, or technical-liaison capability
- **Core message:** practical judgement at the point where people, process, technology, evidence, and decision-making meet
- **Public status:** suitable for public use once pages, case studies, and contact routes are checked

## What this site should demonstrate

This site should show evidence of:

- clear professional positioning
- structured communication
- operational and process thinking
- ability to document work in a way non-technical people can understand
- responsible use of AI as a support tool, not as a substitute for judgement
- practical understanding of websites, GitHub, version control, deployment, content structure, and digital governance
- public-facing presentation without Silicon Valley-style exaggeration

## Portfolio rule

Every public project or case study linked from this site should answer five questions:

1. What problem or need was being addressed?
2. Who was the intended audience or user?
3. What information, evidence, or constraints shaped the work?
4. What tools or methods were used?
5. What does the work demonstrate about Alan's judgement, organisation, communication, or delivery ability?

## Repository management

- `README.md` explains the purpose of this repository.
- `PROJECT-STATUS.md` records the current portfolio classification and clean-up standard.
- Public pages should remain written in plain British English.
- No confidential, private, employer-sensitive, or third-party personal information should be committed.
- Work-in-progress pages should not be promoted from the live portfolio until they have a clear purpose, audience, and status.

## Showcase standard

Before this site is used prominently in job applications, it should have:

- a clear homepage message
- a focused CV/profile page
- a small number of strong project examples
- contact details or a safe contact route
- consistent visual style
- checked links
- checked spelling and punctuation
- no private or unnecessary personal material

## Working principle

This site should present technical fluency as an operational strength: Alan can understand tools, ask better questions, organise information, translate requirements, work with AI and technical systems, and help people make clearer decisions.
